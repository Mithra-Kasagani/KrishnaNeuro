## Questions & Answers

**Q: Do I need to know my diagnosis before booking?**  
A: No. Tell the clinic what has changed. The consultation can clarify possible causes, urgency and next steps.

**Q: Does a first consultation always mean medication?**  
A: No. Recommendations depend on the assessment and may include education, practical changes, therapy, monitoring, medication or referral.

**Q: What should I bring?**  
A: Bring current medicines, available past prescriptions or reports, a brief timeline and your main questions. Do not delay urgent care to collect paperwork.

**Q: Can a family member attend?**  
A: Yes, if the patient wishes. Family information can help, while part of the consultation may remain private.

**Q: Do you see children and teenagers?**  
A: Child and adolescent concerns are included. A parent or legal guardian is normally involved; call to confirm the appropriate appointment.

**Q: Is teleconsultation available?**  
A: It may be suitable for selected situations or follow-ups, subject to clinical suitability and applicable standards. Call to confirm.

**Q: What are the timings?**  
A: The supplied clinic brochure lists Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–9:00 PM, with Sunday closed. Call before travelling because schedules can change.

**Q: Is the clinic a 24-hour emergency department?**  
A: No. For immediate danger, overdose, seizure, severe violence or inability to stay safe, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department. Tele-MANAS: 14416.

7. **Sleep tablets are not the whole sleep plan** — Chronic insomnia often needs a structured assessment and behavioural approach. **CTA:** Learn more.
8. **Memory change is not automatically dementia** — Mood, sleep, medicines and medical problems can contribute. Sudden confusion is urgent. **CTA:** Book.
9. **Postpartum mental health matters** — Persistent low mood is treatable. Confusion, no need for sleep, voices or unusual beliefs after childbirth is an emergency. **CTA:** Read guide.
10. **Medication should have a purpose** — Ask what it targets, how benefit will be measured, which effects need monitoring and when it will be reviewed. **CTA:** Learn more.
